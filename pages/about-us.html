About CDREA

Centre for Development Research, Enlightenment and Advocacy

Who We Are

The Centre for Development Research, Enlightenment and Advocacy (CDREA) is a non-political, non-profit organization established on January 1, 2018, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under registration number CAC/IT/NO.109189.

Our Vision

To see a society where there is peace and justice, where women, youth and persons living with disability can exercise their human rights.

Our Mission

To enhance democratic values, expel ignorance and poverty through developmental programs.

Where We Work

CDREA operates primarily in the Niger Delta/South-South region of Nigeria, with active programs in Akwa Ibom State (including Okobo and Mkpat Enin Local Government Areas) and past collaborations in Rivers State.
