Get Involved

There are many ways to support CDREA's mission of strengthening good governance and civic participation in Niger Delta.

We Need Your Help

CDREA works directly with local citizens, civil society organizations, and government chairmen across Akwa Ibom State. Our target beneficiaries include men, women, hildren and youths (50% Women, Children and youth) and persons living with disability.

Whether through volunteering time and expertise, or supporting our programs financially, your contribution helps sustain civic education, capacity-building, and accountability initiatives at the local level.
