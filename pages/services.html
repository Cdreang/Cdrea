Our Programs

Strengthening good governance and civic participation at the local level

Current Project

Strengthening Good Governance and Citizens' Participation in Democratic Processes

This project works to increase the capacity of key stakeholders — local government chairmen, civil society organizations (CSOs), and local citizens — to fulfill their roles and responsibilities, ensuring genuine accountability, transparency and effective service delivery.

The project covers 10 communities across 2 Local Government Areas (Mkpat Enin and Okobo) in Akwa Ibom State, directly reaching 1,500 citizens (50% women and youth) and indirectly benefiting an estimated 15,000 community members, including persons living with disability and other marginalized groups.

Our approach includes:

  • Advocacy engagement with key stakeholders
  • Capacity building for community members and CSOs
  • Round-table meetings with the media
  • Radio jingles in local dialects and English
  • Establishing a Community Participatory Management Committee (CPMC) to sustain a tripartite relationship between local government chairmen, CSOs, and citizens

Past Work

Women United for Economic Empowerment (UNDEF-funded)

An 18-month project (July 2020 – December 2021) that promoted transparency, accountability, good governance and effective public service delivery at the local level in Akwa Ibom and Rivers States. The project reached 54 local government chairmen and 20 local civil society organizations, with 20,000 local citizens as target beneficiaries.

Key results achieved:

  • Increased citizen participation in the electoral process following civic education training
  • Two-day capacity-building workshops held for 54 local government chairmen (31 from Akwa Ibom, 23 from Rivers State), with 70% committing to good governance practices
  • Improved understanding of good governance and accountability among trained civil society actors
  • Increased transparency, accountability, and coordination between local government stakeholders
